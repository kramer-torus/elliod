# Elliod training plan

A 14-week hybrid block for a 36-year-old male, 185 cm, 80 kg, coming off a
3:23 marathon (12 weeks at 3–5 runs/week, ~200 km/month, little lifting).

Goals, in priority order for this block:

1. Get stronger, with shoulders and posture leading a rounded programme.
2. Lose fat (target ~75–76 kg) without losing the ability to run well.
3. Keep running ability: hold 5k–10k speed and a comfortable 16–18 km long run.

## The trade-off, stated plainly

Maintaining marathon fitness, building strength and cutting fat pull against
each other. Any two are straightforward; all three at once means each gets a
sustainable dose, not a maximal one.

- Running volume drops from ~46 km/week to 32–40 km/week (3 runs). That holds
  aerobic fitness and speed with one quality session plus a long run. It does
  not hold marathon-specific endurance indefinitely; a marathon would need a
  proper build afterwards.
- Strength is the most likely thing to improve. Coming off a block with little
  lifting, novice gains are available even in a deficit.
- Fat loss is capped at ~0.4–0.5 kg/week. Faster and the runs flatten and lifts stall.
- Weeks 1–2 are recovery, not optional. Marathon muscle damage outlasts the
  feeling of being recovered by two to three weeks.

## Weekly template

| Day | Session |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | **Upper A** — barbell overhead press, incline DB press, chest-supported row, face pulls, lateral raises, scapular pull-ups + dead hang, Pallof press |
| Tue | **Quality run** — threshold or VO2 intervals |
| Wed | **Lower** — odd weeks: trap-bar deadlift, Bulgarian split squat, hip thrust, Nordics, calf raises, Copenhagen plank. Even weeks: front squat, RDL, step-ups, Nordics, calf raises, hanging leg raise |
| Thu | **Easy run** + 6 × 20 s strides |
| Fri | **Upper B** — weighted chin-ups, half-kneeling landmine press, single-arm DB row, rear-delt fly, prone Y-T-W, farmer's carry, band pull-aparts |
| Sat | **Long run** — 14–18 km, with marathon-pace segments from week 9 |
| Sun | Rest + 5-minute posture routine (optional 4–6 km recovery run) |

Hard days are clustered (Tue quality, Wed heavy legs) so easy days stay easy
and the long run follows two lighter days.

### Daily posture routine (5 minutes)

Thoracic extension over a foam roller ×10 · wall slides ×10 · band pull-aparts
×20 · half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch 45 s/side · dead hang 30 s.

## Phases

| Weeks | Phase | Running | Lifting | Nutrition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Recovery | Easy only, 19–23 km | 2 sets/exercise, RPE 6, find starting weights | Maintenance |
| 3–6 | Foundation | Threshold Tue (3×8 → 2×15 min), long 14–16 km; wk 6 deload | 3×8–12 RPE 7.5, double progression | −400 kcal/day; ease on deload |
| 7–10 | Build | Intervals alternate with threshold (5×1000 → 6×1000), long 16–18 km, first MP segment wk 9; wk 10 deload | Main lifts 4×5–8 RPE 8 | −400 kcal/day, carbs up around long run |
| 11–14 | Consolidate | Threshold 3×12, 5×1200, mixed session; long runs with 6–10 km at MP; wk 14: 5 km time trial | Main lifts 5×3–5 RPE 8.5; wk 14 deload + rep-out tests | −400 kcal/day, then 2 weeks at maintenance |

Weekly running volume: 19, 23, 32, 34, 36, 27, 36, 37, 39, 28, 39, 37, 38, 23 km (448 km total).

## Lifting rules

- Double progression: when every set reaches the top of the rep range, add the
  exercise's increment (2.5 kg main lifts, 5 kg deadlift/hip thrust, 1–2 kg
  dumbbells). A set below the bottom of the range → back off 5 %.
- RPE 8 = two clean reps left. Main lifts at RPE 7.5–8.5; accessories closer to failure.
- Deloads (weeks 6, 10, 14): one set fewer, two RPE points lighter.
- Pulling volume is roughly twice pushing volume and every pull is paused —
  that is the posture work. Nordics and calf raises are injury insurance, not optional.

## Nutrition

- Maintenance ≈ Mifflin–St Jeor × 1.35 + training load (≈ 1 kcal/kg/km of
  running, ≈ 250 kcal per lift). For this profile ≈ 2,900 kcal/day.
- Cutting phases: −400 kcal/day → ≈ 2,500 kcal. No deficit on long-run day;
  add ~60 g carbs the evening before it.
- Protein 2.0 g/kg (~160 g). Fat ≈ 0.8 g/kg. Carbs fill the rest, mostly
  around Tuesday and Saturday.
- The estimate is ±10–15 %. Log morning weight most days; after two weeks the
  app compares the trend to the expected −0.36 kg/week and nudges intake.

## Week 14 tests

5 km time trial (compare to the prediction), rep-outs on press, deadlift,
squat and chin-up (the app estimates 1RM), bodyweight, waist, photo. Then two
weeks at maintenance before choosing the next block.

## When to deviate

- Sick or slept under six hours: make the quality run easy or skip the lift. Never both hard.
- A niggle that changes your stride ends the run. Lifting can usually continue.
- Loads going backwards two sessions in a row = under-eating. Add 200 kcal for a week.
- Short on time: keep Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday. Everything else is negotiable.
